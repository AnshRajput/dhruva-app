## Full description

Dhruva AI runs large language models directly on your phone. Every reply is generated by your own device: no server in the loop, no account, nothing to sign in to. Download a model once, switch on airplane mode, and keep going.

ONE-TAP WORKFLOWS
Paste text, open a file, or share it from any app, then tap: Summarize · Key points · Explain simply · Translate (English, Hindi, Hinglish and more) · Rewrite (formal, friendly, shorter) · Fix grammar · Draft a reply · Extract to-dos · Study notes · Quiz me · Social caption · Understand a document.

Perfect for the things you shouldn't paste into a cloud chatbot: a medical report, a bill, a legal notice, a message you're trying to word carefully.

WORKS FROM ANY APP (Android)
Select text anywhere → "Ask Dhruva AI". Or share text to Dhruva. Long-press the icon for New chat and Workflows shortcuts.

EVERYTHING ELSE
• Offline streaming chat, with visible reasoning for thinking models
• Curated model catalog labelled by size and whether your phone can run it, plus all of Hugging Face, plus import your own .gguf file
• Characters with their own personalities and chat history
• Voice both ways: on-device speech-to-text (incl. Hindi/Hinglish) and text-to-speech, hands-free mode
• Vision: ask about a photo or screenshot with an on-device vision model
• Playground: compare two models side by side
• Search all your chats; export as Markdown or JSON
• Report any AI reply in one tap

PRIVACY, CONCRETELY
• All inference happens on your device
• No analytics, crash reporters, tracking SDKs or ads
• No account, no sign-in, no profile
• Network use: model downloads you start, plus app-update checks (app version and patch info only, never your chats)
• Apache-2.0 open source, so every claim is auditable

WHAT YOUR PHONE NEEDS
64-bit device, Android 8.0+ or iOS 14+. Rough guide: ~1B-parameter models need 4 GB RAM, 3–4B need 6 GB, 7B+ need 8 GB. Small models are fast on most modern phones and great at workflows.

ध्रुव (Dhruva) is the pole star. This app is independent and not affiliated with AI4Bharat's Dhruva speech platform.

## App Store review notes

Dhruva runs open-weight language models fully on-device via llama.cpp. No
account or login is required. To test: open the app → Models → download
"Qwen2.5 0.5B Instruct" (the smallest, ~400 MB) → Chat → tap a suggested prompt, or open Workflows
→ Summarize. Downloaded model files are inert data (GGUF weights) executed
by the app's own bundled engine; no code is downloaded (2.5.2). Every AI
reply has a Report action (Settings → Safety & reports lists reports and the
developer contact) per guideline 1.2. Microphone/camera/photos are used
only for user-initiated voice and image questions, processed on-device.

**Age rating:** answer "Unrestricted web access: No"; for AI chatbot
content, choose infrequent/mild mature themes → expect 13+/16+. Play
content rating stays as submitted (Teen), target audience 18+.
